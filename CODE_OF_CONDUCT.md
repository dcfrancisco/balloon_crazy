Be kind and respectful

This project follows a simple Code of Conduct to keep the community welcoming.

Expected behavior
- Be respectful and considerate in all discussions.
- Assume good intent and give helpful, constructive feedback.
- Keep language professional and inclusive.

Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks will not be tolerated.
- Do not share personal data or private information without consent.

Reporting
- If you encounter unacceptable behavior, please open an issue or contact the repository owner directly.

Enforcement
- Maintainers may warn contributors, close issues/PRs, or block users if behavior violates these rules.

This is a short summary. For larger projects consider adopting the full Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org) and linking to a versioned copy.
